♦ 17. oghavaggo ♦ 1-10. oghādisuttadasakaṃ (SN 48.74)

♦ 641-650. “pañcimāni, bhikkhave, uddhambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni. katamāni pañca?

rūparāgo, arūparāgo, māno, uddhaccaṃ, avijjā —

imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcuddhambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni.

imesaṃ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṃ uddhambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyojanānaṃ

abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya pañcindriyāni bhāvetabbāni. katamāni pañca?

idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu saddhindriyaṃ bhāveti

rāgavinayapariyosānaṃ dosavinayapariyosānaṃ mohavinayapariyosānaṃ.

vīriyindriyaṃ ... pe ... satindriyaṃ... samādhindriyaṃ ...

paññindriyaṃ bhāveti rāgavinayapariyosānaṃ dosavinayapariyosānaṃ mohavinayapariyosānaṃ.

imesaṃ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṃ uddhambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyojanānaṃ

abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya imāni pañcindriyāni bhāvetabbānī”ti.
