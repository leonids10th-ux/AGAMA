♦ “addhā kho tyāhaṃ, gāmaṇi, na labhāmi — ‘alaṃ, gāmaṇi, tiṭṭhatetaṃ; mā maṃ etaṃ pucchī’ti.

api ca kho tyāhaṃ byākarissāmi.

yo so, gāmaṇi, assāroho saṅgāme ussahati vāyamati

tassa taṃ cittaṃ pubbe gahitaṃ dukkaṭaṃ duppaṇihitaṃ —

‘ime sattā haññantu vā bajjhantu vā ucchijjantu vā vinassantu vā mā ahesuṃ iti vā’ti.

tamenaṃ ussahantaṃ vāyamantaṃ pare hananti pariyāpādenti,

so kāyassa bhedā paraṃ maraṇā parajito nāma nirayo tattha upapajjati.

sace kho panassa evaṃ diṭṭhi hoti —

‘yo so assāroho saṅgāme ussahati vāyamati,

tamenaṃ ussahantaṃ vāyamantaṃ pare hananti pariyāpādenti,
